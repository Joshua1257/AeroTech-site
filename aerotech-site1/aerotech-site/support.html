Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Use the Track Order link in the Quick Links box. You will need your order number and the email you used at checkout.

What is your return policy?

You can return unused parts within 30 days. Keep the original box and packaging so we can send you a refund.

How does the Build Checker work?

Pick a part for each slot and the checker compares sockets, memory type, case size and power. It tells you if anything will not fit.

Is there a warranty on parts?

Yes, all parts come with the manufacturer warranty. Graphics cards and power supplies have at least 2 years.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders arrive in 2-4 working days. Orders over $99 ship for free.
